About

Law Offices of Vincent Kim

Attorney Vincent Kim (김형걸) has handled green cards, visas, citizenship and removal defense from Wilshire Boulevard in Los Angeles for twenty years. Since 2015 he has written about immigration law for the Korean community online, and he has appeared on KBS America News to explain policy changes.

Key services

  • Spouse & family petitions
  • Employment-based EB-2 · EB-3
  • National Interest Waiver (NIW)
  • EB-5 investor green cards
  • Removing conditions (I-751)
  • H-1B work visas · L-1 transfers
  • E-2 treaty investor visas
  • F-1 student visas · OPT
  • Naturalization (N-400)
  • Immigration court defense
  • Waivers (I-601 · I-601A)
  • Denials, RFEs and past records
