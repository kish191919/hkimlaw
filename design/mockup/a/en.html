About the attorney

Vincent Kim

  • 20 years in U.S. immigration law
  • 1,500+ articles on immigration law (Naver Blog, in Korean)
  • KBS America News appearances (2015–2026)

Attorney Vincent Kim (김형걸) has practiced U.S. immigration law for twenty years from his office on Wilshire Boulevard in Los Angeles.

Since 2015 he has answered immigration questions in a Korean-language online community with more than 2,100 members, and he has appeared on KBS America News to explain changes in immigration policy.

He believes good decisions start with knowing exactly what a new rule means for your own case, so he explains both the options and the risks before you decide.
